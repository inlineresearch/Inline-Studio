# Code of Conduct

We want Inline Studio to be a welcoming place for everyone who makes, tinkers, or files a bug. This
is a short version of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).

## Our standards

Things that make this community work:

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, skill levels, and experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people in it.

Things that are not welcome:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in our Discord, and when
someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour in our [Discord](https://discord.gg/cSUS88VdY9), or privately to
**team@inlinestudio.art** for anything sensitive. Reports are handled discreetly. Maintainers may
warn, remove content, or ban anyone who breaks these standards.
